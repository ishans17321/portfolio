How to Pat a Cat

Have you ever wanted to pat a cat?

Follow these steps gently, and let the cat choose whether to stay.

How to pat a cat

Stop if the cat moves away or seems uncomfortable.

  1. Put out your hand slowly and let the cat approach.
  2. Gently touch the cat if it welcomes contact.
  3. Move your hand back and forth softly, checking its reaction.

Congrats! You pat a cat

A short, calm interaction is enough.
